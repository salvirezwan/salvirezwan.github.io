About me

Who Am I?

Hi! I am Rezwan Islam Salvi, currently working as an AI Engineer at AttenSys AI. I am a Computer Science and Engineering Graduate from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), Dhaka, Bangladesh. With a strong passion for Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing, I love to work on innovative solutions that drive meaningful impact. I am skilled in Python, C++, and SQL, and I continuously strive to expand my expertise in solving complex problems efficiently. I work with PyTorch, TensorFlow, LangChain, LangGraph, Scikit-learn, and SentenceTransformer for Machine Learning and LLM applications. I use OpenCV, Numpy, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn for Data Analysis and Computer Vision. On the backend, I build APIs with FastAPI and Flask, using Pydantic for structured models, and I work with databases like MongoDB and Vector stores like ChromaDB. I also create automated workflows with n8n, and build interactive demos using Gradio and Streamlit.
